Tamara Tirják spent a decade leading global content at Frontier Developments, the studio behind Planet Zoo, Planet Coaster, and Jurassic World Evolution. The team invented a fictional language and ran 48-hour content pipelines for live-service games. She discusses why gaming audiences are the least forgiving in the world when content fails to connect.
