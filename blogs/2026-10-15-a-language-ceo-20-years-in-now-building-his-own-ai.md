Sean Hopwood founded Day Translations in 2007 without a computer or taking outside investment. Nearly two decades later, the company works with more than 10,000 linguists across three continents. In this CEO-to-CEO edition, Sean joins Phrase CEO Georg Ell to share his experience of staying independent in a consolidating industry and building his own AI.
