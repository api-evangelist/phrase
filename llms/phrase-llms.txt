# Phrase
> Phrase is a language intelligence platform that automates, manages, and delivers multilingual content for deeper customer connections and faster business growth.

Phrase helps global teams streamline localization workflows, improve translation quality, and scale multilingual content across products, marketing, and customer experiences.

## Products
- [Phrase Platform] (https://phrase.com/platform/): The Phrase Platform unifies translation management, software localization, and content workflows in one place.
- [Phrase Strings](https://phrase.com/platform/strings/): Localization solution for developers to manage and automate software translations.
- [Phrase TMS] (https://phrase.com/platform/tms/): Translation management system for enterprises to manage localization at scale.
- [Phrase Orchestrator] (https://phrase.com/platform/orchestrator/): Automation layer to orchestrate localization workflows across systems and teams.
- [Phrase Analytics] (https://phrase.com/platform/analytics/): Provides insights into localization performance, costs, and efficiency.
- [Phrase Studio] (https://phrase.com/platform/phrase-studio/): Capture, translate or convert video or audio into global content
